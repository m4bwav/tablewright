# Minis

Load when a character or creature should become a printed miniature.

- Scale: heroic 28 to 32 mm figures on 25 mm round bases for Medium creatures (one 5 ft square), 50 mm for Large, 75 mm for Huge.
- From pictures: a front reference on a plain ground, plus side and back views painted from renders of a first mesh so gear cannot move between views. Mesh with an image-to-3D model, scale the figure, union it with the base into one watertight solid. The sf2e-miniature skill does all of this for Starfinder characters.
- Printing: hand the solid to polywright-print (FDM and resin rules, supports, splitting) or printable-3d-model (Bambu P1S settings). On FDM, 0.08 to 0.12 mm layers and tree supports; thin weapons under 1 mm break, so thicken them.
- Check the preview before printing: gear sticking out sideways means the side views disagreed; rebuild from the front view alone.
