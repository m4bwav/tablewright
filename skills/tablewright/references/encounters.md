# Encounter math

Load when building or judging an encounter, or when a number looks wrong.

## Pathfinder 2e and Starfinder 2e (ORC)

Budget for 4 players: trivial 40, low 60, moderate 80, severe 120, extreme 160. Each player above or below four adds or removes 10, 20, 20, 30, 40 respectively.

Creature XP by creature level minus party level: -4 is 10, -3 is 15, -2 is 20, -1 is 30, 0 is 40, +1 is 60, +2 is 80, +3 is 120, +4 is 160. Lower than -4 does not count; higher than +4 should not be used against that party. Hazards use the same table (simple hazards cost a fifth).

Source: Pathfinder GM Core and Starfinder Player Core encounter building rules, as published on Archives of Nethys (link it, do not copy it).

## Beyond the number

Many weak foes act more often than one strong one; a solo boss at +3 or +4 (PF2e) lands crits; ranged foes behind cover and difficult terrain raise the real threat. Mention these before the band.
