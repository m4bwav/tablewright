# tablewright

Run as `python "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/tt.py" <command>`. Private data (characters, which often carry players' and children's names, campaign notes, tracker files, portraits, minis) lives in `TABLEWRIGHT_HOME` (default `~/.tablewright`), never in this plugin.

## Encounters

1. Get the system, party size and party level. Foes are creature levels (pf2e, sf2e) or challenge ratings (5e).
2. `tt.py encounter --system pf2e --party 4 --level 3 --foe 5 --foe 2 --foe 2` gives total XP, threat band and what the next band needs. It exits 1 when a foe is more than 4 levels above the party. Add `--mission rescue --clock 4 --healers 1` to get warnings for a kill-only goal, a goal with no round limit, a clock outside 1 to 5 rounds, and foes that heal.
   Reinforcements: `--wave 3:2x1` (round 3, two level-1 foes; add `:bridge` if the prep step `bridge` removes it), `--pool N` warns when waves draw past the defenders, `--prep bridge` marks done prep (warns when it removes no wave), `--morale half` says when foes retreat (warned when waves have none), `--smart` warns without `--escape`. `--hazard L` adds hazards; identical ones count barely more than one.
3. `tt.py budget --system 5e --party 4 --level 5 --threat moderate` gives the XP to spend and sample foe counts. Pick real creatures from the system's own books or the person's notes; tablewright ships numbers only.
4. Say what the band means at the table (PF2e: moderate is a fair fight, severe can kill a careless party; 5e 2024: high can drop a character) and suggest one change to move the band (add or remove a foe, swap a foe for one a level lower). Terrain, action economy and solo bosses matter more than the number: name them.

Tables and sources: [references/encounters.md](references/encounters.md).

## At the table

`tt.py tracker init "Hero:17:20" "Goblin:12:6"` (name:initiative:hp), then `tracker next`, `tracker hit Goblin 5`, `tracker heal Hero 4`, `tracker cond Hero "frightened 1"` (`--remove` to clear), `tracker add "Reinforcement:9:12"`, `tracker show`. Downed combatants are skipped; `+` marks who is on deck so the next player can get ready. `--public` prints the players' board for a shared screen: order, conditions and damage taken, never HP or HP max. The file is `TABLEWRIGHT_HOME/tracker.json` unless `--file` is given; one file per running fight.

To find what slows fights, log a few real ones as CSV `actor,start,end` (seconds) and run `tt.py combat-log fight.csv`: total time, GM share, median and longest turn per actor, and who runs over 90 s a turn (`--budget`, `--gm NAME`). Measure before blaming anyone.

## Session prep

Before a session, write a card in markdown with `## Opening`, `## Leads` (three bullets), `## NPCs` (`Name: what they want now`) and optional `## Fights` (each with `out: ...`, a way the fight can end before anyone dies or flees), then `tt.py prep card.md`. It exits 1 on a missing opening, the wrong number of leads (`--leads N` to change) or an NPC without a goal, and warns on a fight with no way out. Optional `## Hazards` bullets need `warned: where players learn of it`. A fight or hazard marked `lethal` needs a `tell:` (warns) and fails when also `hidden: yes`. Leads tagged `(false)` should be fewer than real ones and carry `ends: how players learn it is a dead end`. A `## Clock` lists `step: what happens` lines plus a `shown:` line saying how players see it tick. Keep the card short: prep what the table will actually touch.

For a setting, write `## Traits` with 6 to 8 bullets, each `trait: the place, NPC or faction where players meet it`, then `tt.py world card.md`; it exits 1 on too few or too many traits or a trait with no anchor. Optional sections are checked too: `## Nations` bullets need `internal:` and `external:` conflicts, `## Societies` need `leader:`, `goal:` and `rule:` (reward or punishment), `## Cities` need `theme:`, `food:`, `shelter:`, `shop:` and `twist:`.

For random tables, `tt.py table travel.csv` (columns `roll,kind,difficulty,avoidable`, rolls like `1-4`) exits 1 on rolls with no row or overlaps, warns when rolls do not run 1 to a standard die (`--die N` to name it; step tables without `kind` work) and warns when fights fill over 35% of the die (`--max-fight`) or an easy fight cannot be avoided. `tt.py item items.json` checks each magic item has a `drawback_tier` at least its `benefit_tier` (minor, moderate, major, legendary, campaign) and warns on campaign-changing drawbacks. Items may also carry `cost_axis` (coin, body, mind, time, social, risk, none), `uses` (at-will, per-day, charges, once), `unpredictable`, `bypasses` (distance, communication, scouting) and `friction`: when any is set, an item above minor with no cost axis fails, a bypass with no friction fails, and unpredictable at will warns. `tt.py table combine a.csv b.csv [c.csv d.csv] --seed N --count K` rolls 2 to 4 tables together (each checked, 6+ rows) and prints K combinations without repeats while the tables allow. `tt.py mystery case.json` (`party_level`, `conclusions` with `id` and `final`, `clues` with `points_to`, `source`, `dc`, `scene`, `suspects` with `culprit`) fails a conclusion with under 2 clues or only clues more than 5 over the typical DC (10 + level/2), and warns on exactly 2 clues, under 3 suspects, or a first scene whose clues all point at the culprit. `tt.py safety plan.json --party party.json` fails with no `cut_call` or a PC's player (`player`, else `name`) missing from `players_confirmed`, and warns on no `brake_call`, `debrief_minutes` under 10, or lines and veils with no `content_warnings`.

Keep a `## Threads` list of promises and planted objects (`- thing: open` or `- thing: paid`); `tt.py threads card.md` lists the open ones latest first, the usual payoff order, and `--finale` exits 1 while any is open. Tag `(opened: session N)` and open ones older than `--stale 3` sessions warn (`--session` sets now). `tt.py session-log --session N` prints a log template (present, summary, NPCs, places, loot, threads, next opening) whose threads feed this check. Villains go one per `## Name` section: `tt.py villain card.md` wants `wants:`, `public:`, `reach:` (why the party cannot just attack), two or more indirect `harm:` lines, and offers written `offer: option (serves: how it helps them) | option (serves: ...)`. `tt.py twist twists.json` checks planned twists (`text`, `axis`, `sign`, `removes`, `workaround`): a twist that takes an ability away needs a workaround, all on one axis warns, none positive warns. `tt.py campaign plan.json` checks a campaign: every PC in `pcs` has an adventure on their `plotline`, each adventure has `phase` and `why`, no NPC returns after an adventure that `writes_out` them, and it warns on no `opposed_by` or `opportunist`, a `major` adventure not followed by an `aftermath`, and `payback` NPCs with no `payoff`. Optional arc: `events` each need `trigger` and `level`; a `climax` needs `trigger`, `level` and `session`, with `signs` (`text`, `session`) all before it (`--signs 3` wanted). City hook decks: `tt.py hooks deck.json` (rows with `text`, `shape`, `district`, `stakes`, `choice`, `followup`, `rumour`, `truth`) wants every tag, two choices on plot hooks, a followup on framed, accused, mistaken or ambush hooks and a truth behind each rumour. `tt.py tavern card.json` checks a tavern by `role` (map_dot, plot_point, encounter, adventure), a `special` menu item, `map` against `expects`, two `factions` in a `conflict`, a `goto` on each hook and a hook `for` each PC; `tt.py venues --population N --taverns T --inns I` says how many fit. `tt.py party party.json` wants each PC tied to two others and given a `plot_tie`, and a `fallback` on every planned reveal. For NPCs, `tt.py npcs card.md` checks three-line cards under `## NPCs`: `- Name: look; doing now; hook because why`.

Comfort levels: a safety plan may add `comfort` ({player: {topic: none|offscreen|limited|full}}) and `planned` ({topic: level}); each topic is set at the lowest player's level, a planned level above it fails, an unrated planned topic warns. Empty `takebacks` or `info_flow` warn. Prep cards may add `## Players` (`Name: hook`) and tag leads, fights, hazards and `## Scenes` bullets with `; for: Name`, `; likely: definite|possible|unlikely` and on fights `; diff: easy|moderate|hard`: a player with no moment warns, no definite item or mostly unlikely ones warn, three or more rated fights with no easy or no hard one warn. A `table` CSV may add a `when` column (moving, camped, any); all one state warns. A `fiveroom` climax may carry `tiers` {`minimum`, `middle`, `full`}; fewer than three, or a minimum that needs a kill, warns.

## Running the table

- Party strength: `tt.py strength 3 4 4 4 --target-level 4` gives the average party level (.5 rounds up) and very weak to very strong from headcount against `--target-size` (default 5) and level against the target; it fails under 3 or over 7 players. A coarse overlay; `encounter` is the real math.
- Loot: `tt.py loot loot.json --seed N` with `gold`, `pcs` (`name`, `permanent` count) and `items` (`name`, `kind` permanent or consumable, `interested`, `agreed`). Gold splits evenly; a permanent item goes to the agreed PC, else the interested PC with the fewest permanent items, ties by a seeded roll; consumables are left to the players.
- First reactions: `tt.py reaction --approach friendly|neutral|hostile --mod N` rolls 2d6 into hostile, wary, neutral, open or helpful. Only for NPCs made on the spot.
- City heat: `tt.py heat crime Watch --witnesses 2 --why "..."`, `heat standing Guild --by -1 --why "..."`, `heat cool --by 1` after a quiet session, `heat show`. The ledger lives in `TABLEWRIGHT_HOME/heat.json` (`--file`). Heat steps: quiet, noticed, collector, enforcers, open hunt.
- Premise cards: `tt.py premise card.json` (`type`, `core`, `twist`, `root`, `hook` {`type`, `root`}, `finish_line`, `factions` [{`name`, `agenda`}], `at_risk`, `faulty_intel`, `choice`) fails on a missing core or twist, a hook layer that is the same premise and root, pursuit with no finish line, a race without an agenda per faction, or an investigation with nothing that can be lost.
- Wandering encounters: `tt.py wander --every 60 --chance 6 --hours 4 --budget 2` says how many encounters to expect; `--placed AREA` reminds you to use the placed population there; `--run --seed N` rolls each check, then distance (2d6 x `--feet`), surprise per side (1 in `--surprise`), reaction, and whether the party can evade before any fight. Every number is ours and configurable.
- Mass battles: `tt.py battle plan.json --results won,lost,drawn` wants exactly three `fights`, each with a `shape` (the moment the PCs play) and a `carry` (what a win gives the next fight); it prints the carries earned and a result from rout to decisive win. Narrate the armies between fights, never the PCs.
- Turn timer: `tt.py tracker init ... --timer 60`, then `tracker late` marks the current player delayed and moves on. The GM may pause the clock while looking things up.
- Session review: the `session-log` template has `## Highlights` (`scene; players; payoff`) and `## Weak points` (`scene; players; problem; fix`). `tt.py review logs/*.md` tallies both per player, lists the fixes and warns on a player with no highlight.
- House rules: `tt.py houserules rules.json` (list of `rule`, `kind` add/change/remove, `why`, `offset`, `review_after`) fails a rule with no why and warns on a removal with no offset or a rule with no review date.
- Skill challenges and long tasks: `tt.py challenge card.json` (`goal`, `successes`, `failures`, `skills` with `kind`, `on_fail`, `shown`; optional `pool` with `milestones` [{`at`}], `minutes` and `limit`) fails on no goal, under 3 skills or no failure route, and warns on one skill kind, a hidden score or a time limit shorter than a flawless run.
- Split parties: `tt.py calendar days.json` ({"groups": {name: [{day, event}]}}) prints each timeline and warns when groups drift `--drift 3` days apart.
- Hirelings: `tt.py hirelings roster.json` (`going_rate`, `leader_cha_mod`, `hirelings` with `name`, `pay`, `treatment`) gives each a loyalty score and the check triggers; `--roll NAME --event unpaid|abused|sent-ahead|bribe|leader-down --seed N` rolls d100.
- City districts: `tt.py district districts.json` (`name`, `watch`, `bribe`, `gate`, `curfew`, `owner`) warns on gaps and prints one card each.
- Chases: `tt.py chase --lead 3 --escape 6 --seed N [--table file]` (lines `complication;shift`) runs opposed d6 rounds until caught or escaped.
- Fight pressure in `prep`: tag fights `treat:` (an edge to find) or `timer:`; a harder fight or climax should turn two of damage, disruption (`hazard:`/`terrain:`), duration (`timer:`), or say `levers: a, b`. Fights may add `stakes: position|assets|character|party` (vary them; a climax risks character or party). Session logs take `ending: countdown|hook|choice|ran-long`, `ended_mid_fight:` and `recap:`; `review` counts endings and warns on missing recaps.
- House rules may add `situation:` and `distinguishes:`; same situation ruled two ways warns.
- Traps: `tt.py trap traps.json` (`trigger`, `detection`, `disable`, `effect`, `severity` setback|dangerous|deadly). Downtime: `tt.py downtime acts.json` (`time`, `cost` or `sink`, `outcomes` when `repeatable`, `hook`). Major NPCs (`; major: true`) need `ideal:`, `bond:`, `flaw:` or `secret:`. Non-item rewards in `item` (`reward: blessing`) need `condition` or `expectation`.
- Villains may add `escape:` (or `captured_ok: true`); fiveroom areas with `dynamic: yes` want `response` and `plan`; party PCs with `joined_mid: true` want `entry` (happenstance|rescued|remnant|errand).
- Attendance: `tt.py attendance roster.json --present N --dynamic collective|competitive|individualist` warns at half or fewer and says what absent PCs do.
- Foe roles: `encounter --foe 3:brute`; prints a role count, warns when 4+ foes share one role.
- Overland travel: `tt.py travel --miles 48 --terrain forest --hours 8 [--known]` gives miles a day (pace times terrain), days, +25% on an unknown route and a fatigue point per hour past 8. Multipliers are ours. `--navigate BONUS --seed N` rolls a getting-lost check a day off a known route (half a day lost each failure); `--track-age DAYS [--rain] [--group N]` prints a tracking target.
- Negotiation: `tt.py negotiate --start wary --goal open --exchange 1 --exchange 0` rolls 2d6 plus each exchange's modifier: 10+ moves one step up, 4 or less one step down, never more than one step an exchange; at hostile the talks end. `--rolls` uses given results.
- One-shots: `tt.py oneshot card.json` (`bond` professional|family|forced, `plot` duty|heist|investigation|survival|other, `finish`, `pregens` with `role`, 2+ `questions`, `stake`) fails on no bond, no finish, a pregen with no stake or under two questions; warns on shared roles or a finish that is only loot.
- Total party kill: `tt.py tpk --cause gm-error|bad-luck|player-error [--keep-pcs]` prints the support script and remedy (refight or concede; a short, sure recovery group; let it stand or a costly return). A wipe is never a lesson; those causes are refused.
- Dungeon alert: `tt.py alert init --tiers 3 [--merge 2:guards+kennel]`, `alert raise --why "..." [--by N]`, `alert lower --why "..."`, `alert show`. Each tier prints its effect (wandering checks, sentries, armed traps, merged groups). File `TABLEWRIGHT_HOME/alert.json` (`--file`); one per dungeon.
- Hook backlog: `tt.py backlog backlog.json` (hooks with `text`, `status` live|back-burner|dropped, `worsens`, `tie` pc:Name or past:event, `heard`) warns outside 5 to 15 open hooks (`--min`, `--max`), on no `worsens`, no tied hook, and a live hook heard over 3 times (`--retell`). `--levelup` prints a what-changed prompt per hook.
- More prep checks (0.12.0): a bullet with `roll:` (one check that gates progress; list them under `## Obstacles`) wants `on_fail:` time|alarm|clock|cost|complication; fights want a `memento:` (clue, loss, gain, plea, curse, known-foe) on at least one; an NPC tagged `opposed: yes` wants `where:` with two or more places; a card with `## Rooms` or `## Dungeon` wants `## Rest` with `where:` and `cost:`. Villains want `absent:` (what they do if the party never comes) and two `where:` places. Keep the villain moving off-screen and jump-cut dull travel.
- Campaign spine: `campaign` plans may add `milestones` [{`kind` discovery|reversal|change_in_view, `session`}] (in that order, before the climax) and `org` {`minions` (4+ types), `sergeants` [{`name`, `plan`}]}. Session logs that end mid-fight want `snapshot:` and `restart:`.
- Spells (0.13.0): `tt.py spell cost --tier T --power P [--mastered]` (tier times power, cap 16; mastered halves, floor 1). `tt.py spell check spells.json` ({`known`: {school: count}, `spells`: [{`name`, `tier`, `school`, `targets`, `range` close|near|far|distant, `duration` rounds, `created` YYYY-MM-DD}]}) checks our default caps (tier 1: 1 target, near, 1 round; 2: 3, far, 3; 3: 6, far, 10; 4: 12, distant, 100), a creation cooldown (tier 2 a day, 3 a week, 4 30 days) and that a tier is no higher than spells known in that school (4 at most).
- Effects: `tt.py tracker effect Orc "on fire" 3` counts down a round each new round and ends at 0.
- Reward ladder: `tt.py rewards ladder.json` (`tiers` [{`name`, `cadence_sessions`, `gate`, `triggers`}]): cadence must lengthen each tier, 3+ triggers each, a gate wanted.
- Vendors: `tt.py vendors vendors.csv prices.csv --wage 2` (vendor,tier,reach and item,tier,price; higher reach is harder): every item tier needs a vendor, rarer stock is never easier to reach, prices never drop as tier rises. Prints prices as days of wage.
- Roles: party PCs may list `roles`, with the needed roles in the file's `roles` or `party --roles a,b`; warns on one PC covering all and on a role nobody owns.
- Nested tables: a `table` CSV `sub` column names `file.csv:dN`; the sub-table must have N rows.
- Campaign: `travel: unlimited` needs `travel_limit`; when `travel` or `nemesis` is set, a missing `nemesis` {`name`, `pc`} or one not tied to a roster PC (`roster` or `pcs`) warns. Villains with `drive_die: dN` list numbered actions `1.` to `N.` (else exit 2).
- Mobs: `tt.py mob --count 6 --hp-each 5 7 2 5` (each hit of 5+ drops one; routs at half or fewer).
- Hacking: `tt.py hack --system own|simple|secure --mods wired remote`; easy and hard mods cancel in pairs.
- Settlements: `tt.py settlement card.json` (`size` thorp|hamlet|village|small town|large town|small city|large city|metropolis, `population`, `civic`, `inns`, `function`, `businesses`) checks our band ranges (20, 100, 1000, 2500, 6000, 12000, 25000, more), the civic kit (hamlet+ authority; village+ council, defence, hall; town+ walls and 2+ inns) and function kits (trade hub: lodging, stables, smith).
- XP (0.14.0): `tt.py xp xp.json` (`party` [{`name`, `level`, `xp_to_next`, `alive`}], `foes` [{`name`, `xp`, `faced`, `overmatched`, `outcome` killed|surrendered|routed|persuaded, `group`}], `story`): faced, not overmatched foes count, a routed group once; the story award may not top the foe XP or 1/10 of any PC's `xp_to_next`; the total splits among survivors.
- Morale: `tt.py morale --rating 12 --mod leader=2 --mod losses=-3 --seed 4` (2d10 over the modified rating breaks; `--roll` for a table roll).
- Awards: `tt.py awards s1.md s2.md s3.md` reads `award: player | kind | ability | reason | xp`, `milestone: player` and `coins: player N` lines, oldest log first.
- Loot owner: `loot` files may add `owner` {`intelligent`, `can_use` [item names], `random`}; items may carry `usable` and `magic`.
- Rooms: `tt.py rooms rooms.json` (`rooms` [{`name`, `timer`, `threat`, `treat`, `stake`, `wonder`, `enemies`, `difficulty`, `damage`, `disruption`, `duration` 1-6}]).
- Clocks: `tt.py tracker clock Fuse 3 --unit turn`, `tracker clock tick Fuse --fail`; at 0 the clock fires.
- Session end: `tt.py session-end --seed 3 --die 6 --drop 1 --players Ann,Bo,Cy`.
- Resize: `tt.py resize --level 3 --party 4 --foe 3:soldier --foe 3:controller --to 3` (adds brutes, soldiers; cuts controllers, skirmishers; a lone solo needs a level change).
- Target: `tt.py target pcs.json --smart --seed 1` (`pcs` [{`name`, `marked`, `threat`, `reach`}]).
- Prep pacing: `prep --hours 4 [--learning]` (1 fight an hour, 0.75 for new players; a 4e-grid number, our default), `prep --first` (`## Prologue`, `## Intros` with `bond:`), else `## Recap`.
- Wilderness (0.15.0): `tt.py travel --watches 3 --watch-odds 1/6 [--no-fire] --seed 2` (day roll with a time slot, one check per watch, chance of at least one night event); `tt.py weather w.json --hours 24 --every 4 --seed 1` (`states` {name: {`change`, `next`, `effects`}}, `encounters` [{`name`, `implausible_in`}] to reroll); `tt.py table check t.csv --dice 2d10` (gaps, overlaps, each row's chance); `tt.py table lint enc.json` (each entry needs `hook`, `truth`, `check`, `morale`); `encounter --count-foe 2d4x1` (min, mean, max XP; warns when the max tops the deadliest band).
- Mass battles (0.15.0): `tt.py unit --hp 45 --block 10 --attacks 2 --ac 18 --enemy-hit -2` (unit points, flags hit-only-on-20), `unit --hero 25 --scale 10 --sequences 3`; `tt.py command army.json` (`general` {`cap`}, `divisions` [{`commander`, `leadership`, `units` [{`out_of_radius`, `disordered`, `lieutenant`}]}], cost 1 +1 +1 -1, floor 1); `morale --break 12` (a third of starting units) and `morale --dice 2d6 --rating 1 --bands "rout<=4,fall back<=7,hold"`; `battle sides.json --resolve --seed 1` (`sides` [two {`name`, `units` [{`attacks`, `to_hit`, `ac`, `hp`, `pursue`}]}]).
- Army logistics (0.15.0): `tt.py march --speed 120 [--road] [--forced] --days-marched 8`; `tt.py supply army.json` (`costs`, `units`, `multipliers` {`mounted`, `meat`}, `base_value`, `weights`, `line`, `line_cap`) and `supply army.json --forage levy|loot` (`forage` {`families`, `levy_yield`, `loot_yield`, `loot_loss`}); `tt.py siege siege.json`; `tt.py upkeep army.json --weeks 4` (`treasury`, `income`, `wages`). All costs come from the user's file.
- Monsters and hazards (0.16.0): `tt.py variant v.json` ([{`base`, `axis` [damage_type, immunity, control, dot, ...], `keeps`, `tell`, `counter`, `role`}]; control or dot needs a counter, damage type or immunity a tell, more than one axis warns); `tt.py template --cr 1/4 --delta 2` or `--scale 1.5` (5e ladder 0, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1..30, rounds up, exit 1 below 0, `--template` twice warns); `tt.py poison p.json` (`delivery`, `save`, `interval`, `onset`, `stages`, `end`, `dc` 10-20); `tt.py portal p.json` (`location`, `destination`, `key`, `direction` two-way|one-way|intermittent, `timing`; one-way or intermittent needs `return` or `strand: intended`); `tt.py trap t.json --full` (also wants `avoid`, `reset`, `alarm`); a trap with `kind: haunt` needs 2+ `detection` routes, a non-thievery `disable`, an `anchor` and `rearm` leave|rest|never.
- Table trackers (0.16.0): `tracker poison NAME POISON a,b,c [ROUNDS]` (a stage per interval at the round wrap, ends past the last; `tracker poison cure NAME`); `tracker countdown NAME d6 --owner COMBATANT` (turns down one on the owner's turn, fires at 0).
- Adventures and politics (0.16.0): `tt.py short s.json` (`site`, `system`, `tiers` [{`level`, `party`, `foes`, `cache`}]; prints each tier's XP and threat, warns when the cache does not grow); `tt.py vote council.json [--dc 10] [--odds] --seed 1` ([{`name`, `votes`, `support` for|against|undecided, `hidden`}]; margin added to a d20, odds by 10,000 trials, warns with no undecided faction); `chase --grid 12` (progress track: each side moves 1-3 a round); `tt.py hoard h.json` (parcels [{`type`, `magic`}]: 3-5, a magic item, 3+ types); `encounter --solo` wants `--tactics`, `--fallback`, `--terrain`; over 12 foes with no `--wave` warns (waves of about 8); `--throng 10x2` counts 10 weak foes as one crowd of level or CR 2; `challenge` skills may carry `max`, `requires`, `assist` (scores nothing) and the card `outcomes` {successes: text}; caps that cannot reach the target fail, an uncapped skill that can finish alone warns.
- Towns and trips (0.17.0): `settlement` takes optional `government`, `defence` {`patrol`, `muster`, `allies`}, `commerce` {`banned`, `black_market`}, `lodgings` [{`name`, `price`, `near`, `hook`}] and `sights` [{`name`, `npc`, `hook`}]; a small town or bigger missing government, defence or commerce warns, so do a patrol with no muster, banned goods with no black market, lodgings all at one price or with no hook, and a sight with neither npc nor hook. `tt.py journey tables.json --kind simple|complex --leads 3 --seed N` rolls your own tables (destination with `places`, patron, motive, purpose or the six `mission` parts, `arrival`); rows may carry `risk` low|mid|high and `clashes` (rerolled); `references/journey-example.json` is a neutral example. `journey --check leads.json` warns under 2 leads, no or uniform risk, no moral or reward, a simple purpose with no `constraint`, payment as the only arrival; a complex lead missing objective, location or threat fails. `tt.py trade --price P --hexes N [--step 5] [--band down20,...,up20 --weights 2,4,8,4,2] [--capacity C --sold S] [--regional]` compounds one d20 step per step hexes (our flatter band), cuts a batch into a filled market to 80%, and adds no markup without `--regional`. `travel --oddity events.json` draws one colour event a journey, never repeated unless `repeat`, written back to `used`; `slow_offroad: 0.5` halves miles off road.

## Adventure structure

For a one-session site adventure, plan it as JSON areas with roles in order: entrance, puzzle, setback, climax, reward (a role is a step, not one map room). `tt.py fiveroom plan.json` exits 1 on roles out of order, not exactly one climax near the end, a puzzle with under two solutions, a setback that costs nothing, or a climax with no non-damage lever; it warns on a missing earlier clue, missing climax terrain, no twist or hook in the reward, areas 1 and 2 in the same mode, and planned minutes outside 120 to 240. Fields: `role`, `mode`, `solutions`, `clue_in` (area number), `fail_cost`, `lock_in` and `reset`, `min_players`, `cost`, `lever`, `terrain`, `immune` and `weakness`, `twist` or `hook`, `minutes`, `xp`; plan-level `party` and `fight_budget`. Puzzles warn without a `fail_cost` and fail when they lock in with no reset or need more players than the party; a climax foe `immune` with no `weakness` warns; a puzzle with `hints` or `reward` warns on no hints, no `baseline:` hint or no reward; setback plus climax `xp` over `fight_budget` warns. Hand the map to mapwright with the roles marked on its rooms; keep loops around the critical path.

## Characters

- Any system: keep a character as JSON in the common schema ([references/characters.md](references/characters.md)), `tt.py check char.json`, then `tt.py sheet char.json --out sheet.md` for a one-page sheet.

- Writing for a child: short sentences, read-aloud friendly, keep their own words from any interview.
